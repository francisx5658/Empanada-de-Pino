Traditional Chilean Food

Traditional Empanada de Pino

During September—and especially on the 18th—I ate many things, such as terremoto (a traditional cocktail), sopaipillas, and anticuchos, among others.

However, the most delicious thing one can eat on September 18th is the empanada de pino; it is a true delicacy. It is not hard to find, as the empanada is a Chilean tradition that can be prepared by your grandmother, your mother, or even yourself, and they are sold all over Chile. The empanada de pino has a golden crust that makes everyone’s mouth water and an aroma blending egg, onion, and meat; some people add raisins, though I personally prefer not to.

In my opinion, the empanada de pino is wonderful—absolutely delicious and authentic. I love the blend of flavors it offers; while not everyone makes them the same way, the ones I ate were truly exquisite.

Final Rating ⭐ 10 / 10
